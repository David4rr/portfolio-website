By constraining the generative model to strict iambic pentameter, the resulting algorithms are not only functional but possess a distinct, rhythmic cadence when read aloud by the compiler.

We sought to bridge the cold logic of computation with the heartbeat of human literature. In the second phase, we introduced noise—a chaotic variable that forced the AI to break its own rules, resulting in beautiful errors. A segmentation fault that read like a tragic romance.

And thus, the digital sonnet was born. Not compiled, but composed.
